# Known limitations

- **Original CAD compatibility:** the N01–N05 archive was supplied after the initial implementation and is being inspected separately. Its variable-thickness, trimmed Bézier/B-spline construction differs from this MVP's current engine. The browser output is not a reproduction of the original variants or a validated anatomical standard. See `LEGACY_ASSET_INVENTORY.md`; original archive/model files are not bundled in the public site.
- **Projected geometry:** calibration establishes a scalar planar conversion under assumptions, not validated physical nail-surface dimensions. No ArUco detection, camera intrinsics/lens calibration, homography correction, automatic segmentation, or true multi-view reconstruction is implemented.
- **Human-assisted contour:** the initial twelve-point shape is a template. Operators must correct and review it; it is never described as automatic detection. Dense contours and unusual topology need future research.
- **Manual curvature:** transverse/longitudinal rise, thickness, asymmetry, extension and tip shape are research inputs. Length/width parameter adjustments are recorded as manual. The full photographed contour is not reconstructed into the CAD contact boundary.
- **Shell approximation:** nominal normal thickness is approximated by fitted offset samples. Sampled separation and topology checks do not prove global minimum thickness or independently exclude all self-intersections. Variable thickness fields, exact normal offset, fastening allowance and printer compensation are not implemented.
- **Other measurement methods:** Touch & Slide and automatic multi-view recovery remain explicitly disabled research alternatives. CSS pixels and devicePixelRatio never provide millimeters. Additional views support human review only.
- **Capture quality:** blur/lighting/near-white-pixel indicators are heuristics with possible false positives. Framing and anatomical suitability require manual review. Upload supports JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 12 MB and 20 MP; convert HEIC first.
- **Local privacy model:** a random HttpOnly SameSite cookie separates browser workspaces; this is not a production account or professional role/authorization system. Losing the cookie loses browser access to its records. No account recovery, encryption at rest, retention policy, backup, or public deployment is provided. Run on loopback; do not expose the development servers publicly.
- **Research statistics:** reference data, effort and repeat groups are operator-entered, not independently authenticated. Repeat-group spreads do not separate pose/operator/device effects. No physical accuracy, acceptance tolerance or fit performance has been established.
- **Runtime scale:** a single local Python process serializes CAD work. SQLite and stored mesh JSON suit a small research workflow; production quotas, background jobs, cancellation and robust crash recovery need separate implementation.
- **Hardware validation:** automated desktop/mobile-sized Chromium checks cannot establish real camera behavior on iOS Safari, Android devices, lens quality, lighting or physical positioning. See `VALIDATION_RESULTS.md` for what was actually exercised.
- **Materials:** all output is a non-wearable laboratory prototype. Anycubic ABS-Like Resin Pro 2 and the finished nail/use system have not been assessed here for human nail/skin contact, adhesion/removal or prolonged wear.

The GitHub Pages edition uses IndexedDB instead of the local SQLite/API service. Records are specific to that browser/device and can be removed by clearing site data; there is no cloud sync or automatic backup. Its STEP exports are valid faceted boundary representations with planar triangular faces, not the Python engine's smooth B-spline surfaces. Mesh checks in the browser do not substitute for runtime OpenCascade verification. See `PAGES_VALIDATION.md` for independently tested examples.
